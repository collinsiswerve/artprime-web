ART PRIME STUDIO

Spaces with soul.
Details with meaning.

We believe a space should feel as good as it looks. Considered materials, quiet contrasts and objects with character come together to create a feeling of belonging.

From the first conversation to the finishing touches, we bring a thoughtful eye to the way you live, work and gather.

OUR PROCESS

Envisioning

We begin with your space, your priorities and your budget. Understanding how you want a room to feel and function gives every design decision a clear starting point.

Designing

Layouts, materials, lighting and furnishings come together into a considered direction. We work through the details with you so the proposal feels practical, personal and complete.

Coordination

Once the design and scope are agreed, we coordinate the selections and finishing details. Clear communication helps carry the intended character of the space through to the work on site.

Styling & finishing

The final layer brings everything into balance: furniture, lighting, textiles and the objects you love. Thoughtful placement turns a finished room into a space that feels like yours.
