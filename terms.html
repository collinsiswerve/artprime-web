Using the website

This website belongs to Art Prime Interiors, based at Spur Mall, Thika Road, Nairobi, Kenya. These terms explain the use of our website and the starting point for enquiries about our products and services.

Please use the site lawfully and do not interfere with its operation, attempt unauthorised access or submit misleading information. If you need help browsing or placing an enquiry, contact us directly.

Enquiries and orders

The website is a catalogue. Adding an item to your cart, sharing a product or opening WhatsApp does not complete a purchase, reserve stock or take payment.

Order via WhatsApp creates an editable enquiry with your selected items and an items subtotal. Before an order goes ahead, we confirm the items, availability, specifications, final price, any additional charges, payment arrangements and delivery or collection details with you. Please check the written confirmation and ask us to correct anything that is unclear before accepting it.

A Studio enquiry starts a conversation about your brief. It does not book a consultation, commit you to a fee or create a design contract.

Products, photographs and prices

Catalogue prices are shown in Kenyan shillings (KES). The cart shows an items subtotal. Delivery, installation and any other applicable charges are confirmed separately before you commit to an order.

We aim to keep prices, descriptions and availability accurate. If we find an error before an order is agreed, we will explain the correction so you can decide whether to proceed. Any change to an agreed order must be discussed with you; a website update does not by itself change an existing agreement.

Colours can look different on different screens, and natural materials can vary. Please confirm dimensions, materials, finishes and suitability with us before ordering. Photographs help illustrate an item or space and do not replace the specifications we agree with you.

Any Studio images labelled as illustrative studies are references for the portfolio presentation, not claims of completed Art Prime commissions.

Payment, delivery and collection

We confirm payment instructions directly as part of your order. The website does not collect card details, payment PINs or online payments.

Delivery or collection arrangements depend on the items and location. We confirm the cost, expected timing, access requirements and any installation work in writing. Please give us accurate contact and delivery information and tell us about stairs, narrow access or other practical constraints.

If an agreed arrangement changes or a delay occurs, we will discuss the next steps with you. Your rights relating to delivery and performance under applicable law still apply.

Changes, cancellations and returns

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as you can. We will explain the options based on the order, its stage and your legal rights. Any specific cancellation, return, deposit or custom-order conditions must be set out before you agree to that order.

If an item arrives damaged, is faulty or does not match what was agreed, contact us with your order details and, where useful, photographs. We will review the issue and arrange the appropriate remedy under the agreement and applicable consumer law.

Custom work may require a separate agreement about approvals and changes once production begins. Your statutory consumer rights continue to apply.

Interior design services

Each design project has its own brief. Before paid work begins, we agree the scope, fees, payment stages, deliverables, approvals, expected programme and responsibilities in a written quotation or project agreement.

Changes to the brief, materials or scope may affect the price and timing. We discuss these with you before carrying out the additional work. Images on this site are not a quotation or a promise that the same materials or layout will suit every property.

Website content and other platforms

Our branding, original text, designs and photographs belong to Art Prime Interiors or their respective owners. You are welcome to share links to our pages. Please ask before reproducing our work for commercial use, and respect any third-party image rights.

Our WhatsApp and social media links take you to other platforms. Those platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their availability or content.

For information about the saved cart, external services and personal information, read our privacy policy.

Your rights and resolving a concern

We want to resolve concerns fairly. Contact us with the relevant order or project details so we can understand what happened and work towards a solution.
