Who we are

Art Prime Interiors is based at Spur Mall, Thika Road, Nairobi, Kenya. We are responsible for the personal information we handle when you contact us, place an order with us or discuss an interior design project.

This policy covers our website and our handling of those enquiries. Services such as WhatsApp and social media platforms also have their own privacy policies.

Information we handle

You can browse the website without creating an account. There is no payment form or customer account system on this site.

  • When you contact us: we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, messages and photographs.
  • For an order or project: we may need your chosen items, delivery address, contact details, measurements, design brief and payment confirmation. Please share only what is needed for the enquiry.
  • When pages load: our hosting, font, catalogue and image providers receive technical information needed to deliver their content, such as your IP address, browser details and the time of the request.

Providing enquiry details is voluntary. We may be unable to arrange a delivery or prepare a useful quotation without the relevant contact, location or project information. Please do not send passwords, payment PINs or full card details.

Your saved cart

The shop saves your selected products and quantities in your browser using local storage, under the name artPrimeCart. This keeps your cart available when you return on the same browser. It has no automatic expiry and stays until you remove the items, clear the site's browser storage or your browser removes it.

The saved cart is not an order and is not automatically sent to us. Choosing Order via WhatsApp passes the item names, quantities and subtotal to WhatsApp in a prefilled message. You can review the message before sending it. WhatsApp receives the link information when it opens; we receive your message when you send it.

We have not added advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics scripts to the website. Local storage is used for the cart. External services may handle technical data or use cookies under their own policies.

Why we use information

We use enquiry and order information to answer questions, prepare quotations, confirm availability, arrange payment and delivery, carry out agreed work and provide aftercare.

Our basis for this processing is taking steps you request before an agreement, performing that agreement and meeting legal obligations, including keeping required transaction records. Where appropriate, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, keeping records and protecting the business, while respecting your rights. Where consent is required, we ask for it and you can withdraw it.

Contacting us about an order does not sign you up for marketing. We do not sell your personal information.

Other services involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes technical requests to serve it.
  • Google Sheets supplies the public shop catalogue. Studio content is published as a file on the website. The public workbook is for product and portfolio content, not customer or order records.
  • Squarespace’s font CDN and image hosts deliver typography and photographs. Our Rauschen A typeface is currently requested from a public Squarespace font endpoint. Depending on the image, the host may be Postimages, Unsplash, Pexels or Cloudinary. These requests can share technical browser information with the provider.
  • WhatsApp, email and social platforms process your communications under their own terms. Our TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X links are ordinary links; we do not embed their feeds or tracking widgets.
  • People helping fulfil an order or project may receive the details needed for their role, such as your delivery contact and address. Information may also be disclosed where the law requires it.

Some providers operate outside Kenya and may process information in other countries. Where we arrange a transfer of personal information, we must use a lawful transfer basis and the safeguards required by Kenyan data protection law. Contact us if you would like information about a transfer relating to your enquiry.

You can read the GitHub privacy statement, Google privacy policy and WhatsApp privacy policy for more about those services.

Keeping information

We keep enquiry and project information for as long as it is needed to respond, complete agreed work, provide aftercare or meet legal, accounting and dispute requirements. The appropriate period depends on the record and our obligations; we do not keep information indefinitely simply because you contacted us.

We limit access to the people who need information for their work and use account and device security measures to protect it. No internet service can promise complete security. Third-party providers manage their own systems and retention periods.

Project photographs can reveal private spaces. If you have a concern about an image or identifying detail in our portfolio, contact us so we can review it.

Your choices and rights

Under Kenya's data protection law, you can ask to be informed about how your data is used, access your information, correct inaccurate or misleading information and object to processing. You can also request deletion, restriction or a portable copy where the law provides for it. If we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send your request to the email address below. We may need enough information to verify that the request relates to you. Some records must be retained to meet legal obligations; we will explain if that affects your request.

You may also raise a concern with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. You do not need to contact us first to exercise that right.

The website is not intended to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has shared personal information with us can contact us to arrange a review.

Contact us

For a privacy question or request, email artprimeinteriorsltd@gmail.com or call +254 748 649 103. You can also find us at Spur Mall, Thika Road, Nairobi, Kenya.
